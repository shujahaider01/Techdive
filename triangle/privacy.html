Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft: this policy describes how the app works today, but it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Review it and add your contact details before publishing.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, username and email address, and your password (handled by Firebase Authentication) or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google.
  • Content you create: tasks, habits, rewards, notes, photos you attach to tasks, messages you send to other users, and the points and achievements you earn.
  • Device information: a notification token so we can deliver push notifications to your device.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: syncing your tasks and habits, showing leaderboards and delivering messages.
  • To send verification emails and notifications you have asked for.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Who can see your information

Other users can see your username and leaderboard standing, and the tasks, habits and messages you choose to share with them. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Triangle uses Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage and notifications) and an email delivery service to send verification codes. These providers process data on our behalf.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your tasks, habits and rewards in the app, change your password in Settings, and turn off notifications in your device settings. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us using the details below.

Children

Triangle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
